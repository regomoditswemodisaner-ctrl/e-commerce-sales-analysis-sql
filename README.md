\# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — SQL



\## Business Problem



An online retail company wants to better understand its sales performance, customer value, product performance, and customer activity.



This project uses SQL to analyze fictional e-commerce data and answer business questions that could support sales and customer-retention decisions.



\## Business Questions



\- How much revenue has the company generated?

\- Which product categories generate the most revenue?

\- Which products sell the most units?

\- Which customers generate the most revenue?

\- What is the average order value?

\- How are customers segmented by spending?

\- How does revenue change over time?

\- Which customers may require re-engagement?



\## Dataset



The project uses a fictional e-commerce dataset containing:



\- 20 customers

\- 20 orders

\- 15 products



The dataset includes customer information, product information, order dates, quantities, prices, and product categories.

\## Key Results



\- Total revenue: \*\*$1,100\*\*

\- Total orders: \*\*20\*\*

\- Total units sold: \*\*41\*\*

\- Average order value: \*\*$55\*\*

\- Electronics generated the highest category revenue at \*\*$600\*\*.

\- Home \& Kitchen generated \*\*$60\*\* in revenue.

\- Customer segmentation identified \*\*10 High Value, 8 Medium Value, and 2 Low Value customers\*\*.



\## Business Insights



The analysis shows that revenue is concentrated across different product categories and customer segments.



Electronics generated the highest category revenue in the fictional dataset, making it an important category to monitor.



Customer segmentation identified a group of high-value customers based on total spending. These customers could be monitored for repeat-purchase opportunities.



The retention analysis provides a framework for identifying customers who have not purchased recently and may require re-engagement.



\## Recommendations



\- Monitor high-value customers and encourage repeat purchases.

\- Investigate lower-revenue products and determine whether pricing, promotion, or product demand may be contributing to lower sales.

\- Monitor monthly revenue and order trends.

\- Develop targeted re-engagement campaigns for customers with longer periods since their last purchase.

\- Continue collecting additional sales data to identify longer-term trends.
